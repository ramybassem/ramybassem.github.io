## Day job: Data Architect

I'm a Data Architect in the airline industry, based in the Columbus, Ohio area. I help organizations unlock the value of their data by making it trusted, discoverable and AI-ready. I think of it as data amplification: turning data from a byproduct of operations into a strategic asset for decisions, automation and innovation.

My background spans enterprise architecture, cloud-native platforms, event-driven and streaming systems, and large-scale data ecosystems. I've spent more than 15 years in integration and architecture, and I enjoy partnering with business and technology leaders to solve complex problems with pragmatic architecture and strong governance.

I work in English and Arabic, with some French.
